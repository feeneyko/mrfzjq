Somniloquium Serenum
The other members of Ave{@nbs}Mujica are trapped in their dreams. Sakiko makes a bet with the 'Master' of the Castle of Dreams, Morpheus, to go into the dreams and try to awaken them, but is overwhelmed by a torrent of ads for Nyamu as soon as she begins.

Fairytales tell of a castle that only children can see, somewhere in Victoria.
Kind fairies live in the castle, treating children to banquets and making their wishes come true with sweet dreams.
But no one knows what the Castle of Dreams really looks like.

Sweet dreams are continuously created and forgotten.
A cycle of creation and forgetting.
