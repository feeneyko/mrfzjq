A Flurry to the Flame
After the Catastrophe, the children gather to listen to the last part of the hunter's legend. The female scholar ponders the village's future. Yato and Noir Corne return to the ruins of Roka and witness the Rathalos's end.
27_g26_dusk_wild
In the end, the hunter didn't defeat the monster.
But the villagers had left, leaving behind only empty ruins to burn in the flames.
Wounded and without a target, the monster roared and bellowed, and returned to the forest on its own.
As though leaving with the parting words:
'I'll be back.'
The hunter stands quietly facing the forest. A long time passes before he answers.
